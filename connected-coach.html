The Cost Of Unrecognised Fear

When Fear Takes Over,
Instinct Disappears.

Fear is natural. But when you don’t recognise it, it can look like preparation.

  • More detail.
  • More meetings.
  • More opposition analysis.

Until preparation becomes an attempt to control a game you’re scared of losing.

You See It In Your Team.

  • Less intensity.
  • Tired minds.
  • Safe decisions.
  • Players who fly in training, then disappear on matchday.

Reconnect With Yourself.
Free Your Team To Play.

Recognise when fear is driving you. Lead with connection and courage.

The Aim

Greater intensity without the ball.
More creativity and courage with it.
