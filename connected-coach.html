Drewe Broughton

I Haven't Just Studied Men.
I've Lived It.

I spent 17 years as a professional footballer, playing more than 600 games.

But the biggest lessons of my life didn't come from football.

I've been divorced and remarried. I'm a husband and a father of four. I've built businesses, lost businesses and built again. I'm an entrepreneur, a coach and a man still doing his own work.

I know what it is to chase success.
To compete.
To provide.
To fuck things up.
To rebuild.
To question who you are when the identity you built no longer works.

For more than 15 years, I've taken that lived experience into my work.

I've coached thousands of men around the world: professional athletes, coaches, leaders, entrepreneurs, fathers and men simply trying to find their way back to themselves.

And underneath the different lives, I keep seeing the same thing:

Men are carrying too much alone.

That's why I created The Connected Coach.

Not because I have all the answers.

Because after nearly half a century of living, competing, falling, rebuilding, coaching and listening to men, I know how powerful it is when a man finally has somewhere to go.
