How Sweat & Courage Works

Resilience. Self-Belief.
Confidence. Consistency.

Every organisation wants these qualities in its people. Developing them starts with understanding the person behind the performance.

Sweat & Courage helps players, coaches and parents explore what gets in the way, and develop the foundations for:

Resilience

Facing setbacks, learning from disappointment and finding the courage to go again.

Self-belief

Building a deeper trust in who you are, beyond results, selection or other people’s approval.

Confidence

Expressing yourself, making decisions and taking action, even when doubt is present.

Consistency

Understanding what helps you perform at your best, recognising what pulls you away and learning how to return.

Through learning, practical resources, honest conversations and ongoing support, we help your people bring these qualities into how they compete, coach and live.

01

What Is Sweat & Courage?

Sweat & Courage is a human performance platform created by Drewe Broughton to bring ongoing learning and support into your organisation.

It explores the human experiences behind performance: fear of mistakes, pressure, setbacks, anger, comparison, trust and relationships.

Our philosophy connects winning and human development. We help people understand themselves more deeply so they can bring greater courage, freedom and conviction into what they do.

02

How Does The Platform Work?

Your members access a dedicated space containing video learning, practical resources, community discussions and opportunities to engage with Drewe.

They can follow a structured course, explore a question relevant to their day or take part in a group conversation.

The platform stays available between training sessions, matches and meetings, helping members return to the learning when they need it.

03

Yes. Your organisation receives a dedicated area within the Sweat & Courage platform, presented in your club or company branding.

For clubs, this includes separate spaces for Coaches, Parents and Players. Each group receives relevant learning within a shared philosophy.

For businesses, the structure can be tailored around leaders, managers and staff.

Your identity creates a familiar home for your people, with the agreed content, communities and support brought together in one place.

05

How Does It Support Coaches, Parents And Players?

Coaches

Explore how your own responses influence the people you lead. Develop greater awareness of fear, control, ego and trust, and learn how to create an environment where people can compete, make mistakes, take responsibility and grow.

Parents

Understand the pressures your child faces and the influence you have. Explore how to support confidence and resilience, respond to disappointment and remain connected through the demands of development and competition.

Players

Understand what happens within you when you compete. Explore doubt, anger, comparison, fear of mistakes and the need to prove yourself. Develop greater trust in who you are and the courage to express yourself under pressure.

Each group works on its own development while building a shared language across the club.

How Do You Develop Resilience, Self-Belief, Confidence And Consistency?

We begin with self-understanding.

What happens when you make a mistake? Who do you become under pressure? How much does your confidence depend on approval? What helps you stay connected to yourself when results go against you?

Drewe’s VASC Method™ (Vulnerability, Acceptance, Sweat & Courage) provides a practical foundation for exploring these questions and taking action.

The wider learning covers eight core themes:

ThemeWhat members explore
Fear & ControlRecognising fear and how the need for control influences behaviour.
EgoUnderstanding the need to prove, protect, impress or be right.
Vulnerability vs InvincibilityFinding the courage to be honest, ask for help and stay open under pressure.
Toxic vs Healthy ShameTaking responsibility for mistakes without making them a judgement on personal worth.
Trust & BeliefDeveloping trust in yourself and others when certainty disappears.
AuthenticityKnowing who you are and expressing that in how you compete, coach and lead.
EmpathyUnderstanding another person’s experience while maintaining standards and boundaries.
Sweat & CourageTurning awareness into action through effort, honesty and a willingness to face discomfort.

Members are encouraged to recognise their patterns, reflect on their choices and practise different responses in everyday situations.

08

How Does The Learning Become Part Of Our Culture?

Through regular use and shared conversations.

A coach might use a short talk to open a team meeting. A player might explore their response to being left out. A parent might reflect on what they say after a difficult match.

A topic such as fear of mistakes can become a conversation across the club: Where are we holding back? How do we respond when someone gets something wrong? What would courage look like in our next performance?

The platform supplies learning and a place to continue the conversation. Your coaches and leaders help bring it into training, competition and daily relationships.

Over time, the philosophy can become part of how your people speak, respond and support one another.
