The Team

The People Behind the Work.

Behind every programme is a team committed to developing people. Meet the coaches and facilitators helping players, parents and leaders know themselves under pressure.

Drewe Broughton

Founder & Performance Coach

Drewe spent almost 20 years as a professional footballer, playing for more than a dozen clubs after leaving home at 16. Behind the results was a private battle with fear, identity and the pressure to perform — the experience that shaped everything Sweat & Courage stands for today.

Now a performance coach, Drewe works with athletes, leaders and organisations to help them perform under pressure without losing themselves, using the VASC Method he built from his own journey.

Ben

Coach & Facilitator

Ben works alongside players, coaches and organisations to embed the Sweat & Courage Framework in everyday performance environments, helping people build the self-awareness and standards to lead themselves and others.

Lucy

Coach & Facilitator

Lucy supports individuals and teams through the human side of performance — developing the courage, honesty and resilience that allow people to thrive under pressure and grow beyond the result.

Ashley

Coach & Facilitator

Ashley helps players, parents and communities apply the Sweat & Courage principles in real life, creating environments where people can perform, develop and support one another honestly.
