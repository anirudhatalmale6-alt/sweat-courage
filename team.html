Lucy Staniforth

Elite Performance Mentor

Former England International · Former Lioness · Emotional Intelligence Practitioner

Lucy Staniforth is a former England international midfielder with more than 15 years' experience competing at the highest level of the women's game. She represented clubs including Sunderland, Liverpool, Birmingham City, Manchester United and Aston Villa, winning the Women's Super League title and making more than 250 senior appearances across club competitions.

Internationally, Lucy earned 17 caps for England, scored twice for the Lionesses, represented England at the 2019 FIFA Women's World Cup, and was part of the squad that won the SheBelieves Cup. Today, Lucy combines her elite playing experience with a passion for helping athletes navigate confidence, identity, pressure and performance on and off the pitch.

Ashley Lawrence

Elite Athlete Mentor

Canadian International · Olympian · Professional Footballer · Lyon · PFA Business School

Ashley Lawrence is one of the most accomplished footballers in world football. A standout at West Virginia University, she became an NCAA All-American before embarking on an elite professional career with Paris Saint-Germain, Chelsea and OL Lyonnes.

Ashley has made more than 150 appearances for Canada, winning Olympic Gold at Tokyo 2020, an Olympic Bronze in Rio 2016, and competing in multiple FIFA Women's World Cups. At club level she has won league titles in France and England while establishing herself as one of the world's premier full-backs. Ashley brings a wealth of experience in leadership, resilience and performing under pressure at the very highest level of the game.
